# EEG101 `/join` Page Integration Brief

## Role and objective

Update the existing EEG101 Join page at `https://www.eeg101.eu/join/` by **adding** a detailed, visual explanation of the e-COST registration and EEG101 Working Group application process. Do not replace, shorten, reorder, or remove the existing Join-page content. The new material should extend the current page and use the same site structure, design system, navigation, footer, and calls to action.

The user-facing explanation must make clear that there are **two key stages**:

1. **Create and activate an e-COST account.**
2. **Complete the profile prerequisite and apply for EEG101 Working Groups.**

The detailed section should sit naturally after the existing e-COST registration item in the current “How to join” flow, or immediately after the existing “How to join” list. Prefer an expandable or clearly separated guide section if that matches the existing site patterns, but keep the content discoverable without requiring a separate page.

## Existing content that must remain

Preserve the current `/join` page, including:

- The “Join EEG101” heading and the statement that membership is free and open to all.
- The existing “What membership gives you” list: COST grant schemes, Working Groups and community activities, Discord, events, training resources, datasets, tools, and opportunities to contribute to the EEG Manifesto and community standards.
- The existing five-step “How to join” flow:
  1. Read about EEG101.
  2. Register on e-COST.
  3. Connect with the community.
  4. Get involved in a Working Group.
  5. Apply for grants.
- The existing Working Groups, grants, Management Committee nominations, eligibility, footer, social links, and legal/supporting links.
- Existing links and calls to action, especially the links to the EEG101 aims and objectives, Working Groups, grants, COST National Coordinators, Action Chair, and e-COST.

The new section is a **detailed implementation guide in addition to** the current overview. It should not imply that the current page is being superseded.

## Recommended new section

### Suggested heading

`Detailed guide: create your e-COST account and apply to EEG101 Working Groups`

### Introductory copy

> There are two key steps to joining an EEG101 Working Group: first, create and activate an e-COST account; second, complete the profile prerequisite and submit the EEG101 Working Group application. The annotated screenshots below show the process from the live e-COST interface. Personal information has been redacted.

Add a two-column or two-card summary:

#### 1. Create the e-COST account

Register the account, enter the applicant’s personal and scientific information, create a password, accept the required COST principles, and activate the account from the email link.

#### 2. Apply for Working Groups

Complete the required primary affiliation, open the EEG101 application, select the relevant Working Groups, write the three short responses, and submit the application.

## Detailed content to integrate

### Stage 1 — Create and activate the e-COST account

#### Step 1 — Open e-COST registration

Open the [e-COST registration page](https://e-services.cost.eu/user/registration/email). Enter the applicant’s primary email address twice. e-COST sends notifications, including the activation message, only to this primary address. Select **Continue**.

#### Step 2 — Complete personal information

Enter the applicant’s title, first name, last name, gender, and birth year. The birth year is required for identification and is not published on the COST website. The form also asks for an ORCID iD, address, country, phone, and mobile phone. ORCID is optional, but it is useful for distinguishing researchers with similar names.

#### Step 3 — Add scientific information

Choose the science field and research area that best match the applicant’s work. For a pharmacoepidemiology profile, the example selection is **Health Sciences** followed by **Epidemiology**. The COST expert-database checkbox is optional. Leave it unchecked unless the applicant separately wants to be contacted for COST expert activities. Select **Continue**.

#### Step 4 — Create a password

Enter the same password twice. e-COST requires at least 10 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. Store the password securely. Do not include it in screenshots or publish it in the guide.

#### Step 5 — Review terms and register

The monthly COST newsletter is optional. The checkbox agreeing to the [Principles Governing COST Activities](https://www.cost.eu/Rules_And_Principles_for_COST_A) is required. Tick the required agreement and select **Register**.

#### Step 6 — Activate the account

After registration, e-COST displays a confirmation and sends an activation link to the primary email address. The account holder must open that message and follow the activation link. If the message does not arrive, check spam or junk folders and use the email address entered in Step 1.

#### Step 7 — Confirm activation before applying

Return to the [e-COST login page](https://e-services.cost.eu/user/login) and sign in after following the activation link. An unactivated account cannot log in or open the Working Group application. If the activation-required message appears, complete activation from the email before continuing.

### Stage 2 — Apply for EEG101 Working Groups

#### Step 8 — Complete the required profile affiliation

After activation, e-COST may redirect the account to the profile overview and require a current primary affiliation before opening the Working Group form. Select **Add affiliation**, search for the applicant’s organisation using at least four letters, and choose the validated organisation record. Select the applicant’s actual work address, then enter the department, job position, and institutional website. Save the affiliation and confirm that it appears as the primary affiliation.

#### Step 9 — Open the Working Group application

Once the account is activated and the profile is complete, sign in to e-COST and open the [EEG101 Working Group application page](https://e-services.cost.eu/action/CA24148/working-groups/apply). The form displays the applicant’s profile details and research area, then asks the applicant to choose one or more Working Groups:

- **1. Reporting Standards:** standardised reporting, templates, pre-registration, and reproducible EEG methods.
- **2. Curation and Harmonization:** FAIR EEG data, harmonised pipelines, and cross-laboratory data comparability.
- **3. Manifesto:** ethical, open, and sustainable principles for EEG research. The EEG101 website presents this work as the EEG Community Framework.

#### Step 10 — Write the three application responses

The form contains three required text areas. Each response has a maximum of 150 words:

- **Scientific Background:** describe the applicant’s relevant scientific background.
- **Motivation:** explain why the applicant wants to join EEG101.
- **Working Group Contribution:** explain how the applicant plans to contribute to the selected group or groups.

Two additional availability checkboxes ask whether the applicant is available to be nominated as an MC Member or to substitute an MC Member or Observer. These are optional and should be selected only when the applicant intends to pursue those roles.

The form offers **Save** and **Save and Submit**. Use **Save** for a draft and **Save and Submit** only after reviewing the Working Group choices and all three responses.

The e-COST form states that the Action Management Committee decides on applications within three months. The official COST Action page also asks prospective participants to read the Action Description and inform the Main Proposer or Chair of their interest. Approval is handled through the COST Action process; account creation alone does not complete EEG101 membership.

### Final review notice — use prominently at the end

> **After you submit:** You must now wait for your application to be reviewed; approval is **not automatic**. COST advises allowing up to three months for a decision. EEG101 aims to review applications much more quickly, so applicants should normally allow a couple of days at most.

Make clear that the “couple of days” expectation is EEG101’s expected operational turnaround, not a guarantee from COST. Keep the official COST timeframe visible as the formal outer timeframe.

### Image implementation requirements

- Keep the screenshots in their original order within the two stages.
- Use responsive images with descriptive `alt` text.
- Preserve the red callout numbers; do not add a second competing annotation layer.
- Do not expose the original unredacted screenshots in the public site.
- Do not publish email addresses, names, postal addresses, phone numbers, ORCID identifiers, activation links, passwords, account IDs, or private application URLs.
- Do not use the password screenshot to imply that the actual password is visible; it is intentionally blank.
- Keep captions short and explanatory, for example: “Step 4 of 5: create and confirm the account password. No password is shown.”

## Suggested information architecture

Use the existing `/join` page as the entry point:

1. Keep the current overview and existing five-step “How to join” flow.
2. Add a visible “Detailed guide” anchor immediately after the current e-COST overview item or after the existing “How to join” list.
3. Present the two-stage summary first.
4. Present Stage 1 with Steps 1–7 and images 1–7.
5. Present Stage 2 with Steps 8–10 and images 9 and 8 in the order used by the text.
6. Place the final review notice after Step 10 and before the existing footer/privacy material.
7. Retain the existing primary “Join via e-COST” call-to-action and link it to the current official application URL.

## Acceptance criteria

The integration is complete only if:

- The existing `/join` content remains intact and readable.
- The page explicitly states the two key stages.
- The detailed account-registration process and Working Group application process are both present.
- All nine annotated screenshots are used or are available in the guide section.
- No confidential information is visible in the public screenshots.
- The final notice states that review is required, approval is not automatic, COST advises up to three months, and EEG101 normally aims for a couple of days at most.
- The page remains responsive on mobile and desktop.
- All links remain valid and open in a sensible way.
- The final page does not claim that account creation alone grants EEG101 membership or grant eligibility.
